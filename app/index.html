About this guide

Vice Trophy Guide is an offline, unofficial tracker and guide for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy is listed with its official name, description and grade, a staged platinum roadmap, and the missable trophies flagged from wording in the official descriptions — a finale, a one-time choice, or a partner who must be present.

Each trophy carries a short guide written in this guide's own words, with practical steps you can follow in game. Guides cite their sources: the Exophase GTA VI trophy page, the PSNProfiles GTA VI trophy list, and the PlayStation official trophy list. No guide text is copied from those sites; they are named so you can cross-check the trophy names, descriptions and grades yourself.

Your progress is stored only in this browser, in local storage — nothing is sent anywhere. Tick trophies off as you earn them and the header tracks your count and trophy points. Use Export progress to download a small JSON backup, and Import progress to restore it on another browser or device.

The spoiler shield hides story trophies you have not earned yet: their names and descriptions are masked everywhere, including the roadmap and the detail view, until you tick them off. Earned story trophies are always revealed, so the checklist never hides something you have already seen in game.

This is a fan-made tool with an original design. It uses no Rockstar logos or artwork and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

About this guide

This is an offline, unofficial tracker and guide for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy is listed with its official name, description and grade, a staged platinum roadmap, and missables flagged from wording in the official descriptions — a finale, a one-time choice, or a partner who must be present.

Each trophy has an own-words guide with practical steps. The sources cited are the Exophase GTA VI trophy page, the PSNProfiles GTA VI trophy list, and the PlayStation official trophy list — no guide text is copied from them; they are named so readers can cross-check names, descriptions and grades.

Progress is stored only in this browser's local storage and nothing is sent anywhere. The header tracks your trophy count and trophy points as you tick trophies off. Export downloads a small JSON backup of your progress, and Import restores it on another browser or device.

The spoiler shield hides unearned story trophies — their names and descriptions are masked everywhere, including the roadmap and the detail view, until you tick them off. Earned story trophies are always revealed.

This is a fan-made tool with an original design. It uses no Rockstar logos or artwork and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
